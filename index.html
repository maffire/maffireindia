Technical Capabilities

MAF Fire employs a highly skilled team of engineers, project managers, supervisors, and certified technicians who bring advanced technical expertise and industry experience to every project. Our capabilities extend across the full life cycle of fire and safety systems—from design and engineering to installation, commissioning, and long-term maintenance. Key strengths include:

We provide complete SITC services for fire alarm systems, fire-fighting networks, suppression systems, security systems, and LPG installations. Our installation processes follow strict QA/QC checks, and commissioning activities are carried out using calibrated instruments and documented procedures to ensure optimal system performance.

All systems are designed and implemented in full compliance with international and regional regulatory frameworks including NFPA, NBC India, Kerala Municipal Building Rules (KMBR), UAE Fire Code, and ISO safety management standards. Our team ensures all project submissions, approvals, and inspections meet relevant authority requirements.

We handle complex, multi-phase projects using advanced planning tools such as MS Project and MAF’s proprietary project management platform. These systems help monitor timelines, resources, workflow stages, procurement activities, and quality milestones—ensuring timely execution and efficient project delivery.

MAF Fire provides comprehensive maintenance services, including routine inspections, preventive maintenance, emergency repairs, and system upgrades. We specialize in modernizing outdated fire detection, suppression, and control systems, ensuring they meet current codes and deliver enhanced reliability and safety performance.

Our experts conduct detailed hazard analysis, risk evaluations, and system requirement studies for buildings and industrial facilities. We ensure designs comply with applicable codes such as NFPA, UAE Fire Code, NBC India, and local civil defence guidelines. Recommendations are tailored to reduce risk, improve safety performance, and enhance operational reliability.

We supply certified fire protection equipment and execute complete installation works including fire alarm systems, firefighting networks, hydrants, sprinklers, gas suppression systems, Emergency/Exit lighting, and LPG systems. All installations follow strict QA/QC procedures to maintain reliability and system integrity.

MAF Fire offers planned preventive maintenance (PPM), corrective maintenance, emergency call-out support, and performance testing for fire protection, detection, security, and LPG systems. We ensure uninterrupted system readiness and compliance with authority standards through scheduled inspections and documented service reports.

Our renewable energy division provides turnkey solar PV solutions including site assessment, engineering, system design, equipment supply, installation, and commissioning. We ensure optimized output, long-term performance, and maximum energy savings for commercial, residential, and industrial clients.

We offer cost-effective Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) covering fire safety systems, security systems, and solar power plants. AMCs include periodic servicing, breakdown support, consumable replacements, inspections, and compliance audits to ensure long-term operational efficiency.

To ensure safe and effective system operation, we provide practical training sessions for facility teams, security staff, and operators. Training covers system handling, emergency response procedures, and best practices for fire and safety management.
